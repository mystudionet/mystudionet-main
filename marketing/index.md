### Why is it better to have one company handle both video production and ad management?
When the same team produces the video and manages the advertising campaign, the creative and the strategy are inherently aligned. The ad manager understands what the video is designed to accomplish and how it was made; the video producer understands the campaign objectives and targets. This eliminates the friction and misalignment that often occurs when separate agencies handle production and media — and allows creative to evolve based on real campaign performance data.

Mystudionet Productions helps Long Island businesses build and execute this type of integrated video marketing strategy.
