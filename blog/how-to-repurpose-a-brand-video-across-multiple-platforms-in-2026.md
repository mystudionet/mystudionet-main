## Why repurpose video instead of making separate videos per platform?

Making one video per platform multiplies production cost by four or five. A repurposing strategy from a single master cut produces 8 to 12 content pieces from one budget. A 60 second brand film becomes a 15 second Meta feed ad, a 30 second Instagram Story, a 20 second YouTube pre-roll, a 30 second TikTok ad, a 60 second LinkedIn Sponsored Video, and organic clips across platforms.

## How do you cut a brand video for each platform?

### Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

The best-performing Meta video ads run 15 to 30 seconds in 1:1 or 9:16 format. Captions are required because most viewers watch without sound. The hook lands in the first 3 seconds visually, before any voiceover.

### YouTube

YouTube pre-roll converts best at 15 to 20 seconds non-skippable, or up to 30 seconds skippable with the brand message before the 5-second skip mark. Standard format is 16:9.

### TikTok

TikTok rewards a natively styled 9:16 cut between 21 and 34 seconds. The opening 2 seconds must be visually or emotionally arresting, with on-screen captions and native or trending audio.

### LinkedIn

LinkedIn Sponsored Video performs best in 16:9 format at 30 to 60 seconds. The majority of LinkedIn video plays silently, so a text hook overlay in the first 3 seconds is essential. Lead with a business result or the problem you solve.

## What is the right order to build a repurposing strategy?

1. Approve the 60 to 90 second master cut.
2. Export a 30 second version for YouTube and Meta feed.
3. Cut a 15 second version for Meta non-skippable and Instagram Stories.
4. Reformat to 9:16 for TikTok and Instagram Reels, adding captions.
5. Keep the 60 second 16:9 cut for LinkedIn and YouTube mid-roll.

Launch on one platform, read the performance data, then expand budget to the platforms that respond.

## Is repurposing the right strategy for every business?

Repurposing works well for businesses running paid ads across two or more platforms, with a product or service that benefits from multiple angles, or planning a campaign for 60 days or more. ### Does showing the same video on multiple platforms hurt performance?

No, when done correctly. Platform algorithms serve ads to different audiences, and a viewer rarely encounters your ad on all four platforms in the same week. Slightly different hooks per platform improve performance further.

### Do I need separate ad accounts for each platform?

Yes. Meta, YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn each require their own ad account, business page, and billing setup. Creative assets are shared, but campaign setup and targeting are platform-specific.

### How long does repurposed video ad creative stay effective?

Most video ad creative fatigues after 6 to 12 weeks at moderate spend levels. A repurposing strategy extends effective life because the same footage can be re-edited with a different hook, giving 2 to 3 effective creative cycles per production budget.
