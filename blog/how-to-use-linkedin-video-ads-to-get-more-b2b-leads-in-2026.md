## Why do LinkedIn video ads work better for B2B than other platforms?

LinkedIn video ads work better for B2B because the platform's targeting is built around professional identity: job title, seniority level, company size, industry, and specific companies. A New York consulting firm can reach CFOs at manufacturing companies in Nassau County or Suffolk County with a message about their exact problem. Facebook and Google cannot match that precision for professional audiences.

The tradeoff is cost. LinkedIn impressions cost more than other platforms. A $10 CPM on Facebook can become $25 to $50 on LinkedIn. But for a $20,000 services contract, the economics still work because the leads are decision-makers, not curious browsers.

B2B businesses that see the best results with LinkedIn video typically share these traits:

- Average deal value of $5,000 or more
- A specific, named audience (not "small business owners" but "operations directors at logistics companies with 50 to 500 employees")
- A video that addresses a pain the viewer recognizes in the first 3 seconds
- A lead form or landing page that captures contact details, not just a company homepage

## How do you write a LinkedIn video ad that gets clicks from decision-makers?

A LinkedIn video ad that converts opens with the specific pain of the target audience, not a company name or logo. Decision-makers skip branded intros. They stop scrolling for their own problems.

The structure that works in 15 to 30 seconds:

- Seconds 0 to 3: Name the exact problem.
- Seconds 4 to 15: Name the cost of the problem and introduce the solution in one sentence.
- Seconds 15 to 25: One proof point or visual demonstration.
- Final frame: A clear call to action with a specific next step.

The video does not require a spokesperson. An AI-produced cinematic visual with a professional voiceover and on-screen text achieves the same result at far lower cost. Mystudionet Productions builds LinkedIn B2B ad packages structured for this format, delivered in 1 to 2 weeks.

## What budget do you need to start a LinkedIn video campaign?

For a first LinkedIn video campaign, a realistic test budget is $1,500 to $3,000 per month. That range generates enough impressions and clicks to draw meaningful conclusions in a 30-day window.

At that spend level, a B2B services business targeting 50,000 to 150,000 professionals in a specific job title or industry can expect:

- 3,000 to 8,000 video views per month
- 30 to 80 link clicks or lead form opens
- 5 to 20 qualified leads, depending on offer clarity and targeting precision

Cost per lead benchmarks: $50 to $200 for professional services, $100 to $400 for enterprise software, and $30 to $100 for local services targeting Long Island or Nassau County.

Production is a separate line item. A professionally produced set of two to three LinkedIn video ads from Mystudionet Productions typically runs from the mid four figures for AI-produced work.

## How do you measure whether your LinkedIn video ads are working?

Four metrics tell the real story:

- Video view rate: percentage of impressions that resulted in a view of at least 25 percent of the video. Below 15 percent means the thumbnail or first frame is the problem.
- Click-through rate (CTR): 0.3 to 0.5 percent is typical on LinkedIn. Above 0.5 percent is strong.
- Cost per lead (CPL): the only number that ultimately matters, benchmarked against your average deal size.
- Lead quality score: manually verify that people filling out your form match your target job title and company size in the first month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should a LinkedIn video ad be?

LinkedIn video ads perform best at 15 to 30 seconds for cold audiences and 30 to 60 seconds for retargeting. Decision-makers on LinkedIn tolerate more content than a consumer scrolling Instagram, but only if the first 5 seconds address a real problem. Keep the cold-audience version short and save the longer format for people who have already seen your brand.

### Do LinkedIn video ads require professional production?

No, but low production quality costs more on LinkedIn than on TikTok or Instagram because the audience evaluates vendor credibility quickly. A poorly lit, poorly edited video signals the same quality as your actual work. AI-produced video, designed and paced correctly, performs as well as traditionally shot content at a significantly lower cost.

### What type of call to action converts best on LinkedIn?

Lead generation forms that pre-fill with the viewer's LinkedIn profile data consistently outperform landing page clicks for B2B offers. An offer with a specific, concrete outcome, such as "Book a 20-minute audit" or "Download the 2026 benchmark report," outperforms vague calls to action like "Learn more" or "Contact us."

### Can a small Long Island business afford LinkedIn video ads?

Yes, if the deal value supports it. A business closing projects at $5,000 or more per client can justify a $1,500 to $2,500 monthly test budget on LinkedIn, especially when combined with retargeting. Tight geographic and job-title targeting keeps CPM down, and strong creative keeps CTR up. Mystudionet Productions can build the creative component from a free discovery call through final delivery.

### How many videos do you need to run a LinkedIn campaign?

A minimum viable LinkedIn video campaign needs two videos: one cold-audience ad that names the problem and the offer, and one retargeting ad that uses social proof or a more detailed case study. Adding a third variant to A/B test the opening line dramatically improves optimization speed and lowers cost per lead over 60 to 90 days.
