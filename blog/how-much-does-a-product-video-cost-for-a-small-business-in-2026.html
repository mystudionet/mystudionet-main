How Much Does a Product Video Cost for a Small Business in 2026?

A product video for a small business in 2026 typically costs from the low four figures to the mid five figures. AI production brings that down to roughly 20 to 40 percent of a traditional shoot, with delivery in 1 to 3 weeks.

Kursad Yonet
September 22, 2026

A product video for a small business in 2026 typically costs between a few thousand dollars and the mid five figures. With AI production, most businesses get broadcast-quality results at roughly 20 to 40 percent of what a traditional crew would charge. The right number for your project depends on format, length, and how many deliverables you need.

Quick Summary

  • A short product demo or social ad (15 to 30 seconds) produced with AI typically runs in the low four figures.
  • A full product video (30 to 90 seconds, broadcast quality) usually falls in the mid four figures to low five figures with AI production.
  • Traditional product video production for the same project often costs $15,000 to $50,000.
  • The biggest price drivers are creative complexity, number of products, platform formats needed, and revision rounds.
  • Most AI product videos are delivered in 1 to 3 weeks, compared to 4 to 8 weeks for a traditional shoot.
  • Mystudionet Productions works with small businesses across Long Island, Nassau County, Suffolk County, and New York City.

What does a product video typically cost in 2026?

In 2026, professionally produced product video costs fall into a few clear ranges based on production method:

  • Short social ad (15 to 30 seconds, AI production): low four figures
  • Full product video (30 to 90 seconds, AI production): mid four figures to low five figures
  • Traditional product video (30 to 90 seconds): $15,000 to $50,000 for a local shoot, higher for national quality
  • Multi-product campaign (several formats and platforms): priced per deliverable set; AI production still comes in well below the cost of a single traditional shoot day

AI production removes most of the logistics expense. There is no rented studio, no crew to schedule, no travel, and no reshoot when lighting does not cooperate. What remains is the work that always mattered most: concept, craft, and precise product representation.

What type of product video does a small business actually need?

The format you choose affects cost more than length does. Common product video formats for small businesses include:

  • Product demo: shows the product in use, typically 30 to 60 seconds, works well on YouTube and Meta
  • Lifestyle video: shows the product in a real-world context to communicate brand feel, 15 to 60 seconds
  • E-commerce product video: a tight, clean presentation for a product detail page or Amazon listing, often 30 to 45 seconds
  • Social media ad: cut for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts, usually 15 to 30 seconds in 9:16 format
  • Launch film: a longer, more cinematic piece (60 to 90 seconds) for a new product introduction

A small business that sells one or two products typically starts with one hero video plus two or three platform cuts. A campaign with multiple products and platform formats costs more but still falls well below the cost of a traditional crew.

What drives the price of a product video up or down?

Five factors move the quote most significantly:

  • Product complexity: a single hero product with clean packaging films faster than a product with many variants or fine details that require close-up work.
  • Creative direction: a basic white-background product demo costs less than a cinematic lifestyle video with multiple scenes and environments.
  • Number of platform formats: one 16:9 video costs less than a full package of 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 cuts.
  • Revision rounds: more rounds of refinement add generation time and edit hours.
  • Voiceover and licensed music: professional voiceover and cleared music tracks add a modest but real cost.

Is AI product video quality good enough for e-commerce and paid ads?

Yes, for most small business applications. In 2026, AI-generated product videos meet the quality bar for Meta ads, YouTube pre-roll, TikTok ads, LinkedIn, Amazon product pages, and website embedding. The gap between a well-crafted AI video and a traditionally produced video has narrowed to the point where most viewers cannot distinguish between them on a phone screen.

The situations where AI production is a weaker fit are narrow: a product where real tactile feel is the selling point (some luxury goods, handmade items), or a shoot that specifically needs a well-known real person on camera. For most retail, beauty, food, health, home, software, and e-commerce brands in Nassau County, Suffolk County, and across New York, AI production delivers results that compete visually with national brands.

Who is a product video a good or bad fit for?

A product video is a strong investment when:

  • You sell a physical product and want to increase conversion rates on your website or product listings
  • You are running paid ads on Meta, TikTok, or YouTube and need creative that outperforms static images
  • You are launching a new product and want to build awareness quickly
  • You operate in a visually driven category: food and restaurant, beauty and fashion, health and fitness, home and garden, or retail

A product video is a weaker fit when the product is highly technical and the buying decision is driven almost entirely by spec sheets rather than visual appeal, or when your entire budget is under the typical minimum for a professional production.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many revisions are typically included in a product video package?

Most professional AI video studios include two to three rounds of revisions in a standard package. Additional revision rounds are usually available at an hourly or per-round rate. Mystudionet Productions discusses revision scope on every discovery call so there are no surprises at delivery.

How long does it take to produce a product video?

With AI production, most product videos are ready in 1 to 3 weeks from approved concept. Traditional production schedules typically run 4 to 8 weeks when you factor in pre-production, the shoot day, and post-production. The AI workflow removes location scheduling, crew coordination, and weather dependencies.

Do I own the final product video files after delivery?

Ownership terms vary by studio, so always clarify before signing. A reputable studio provides full ownership of the final delivered files. Ask specifically whether you receive platform-formatted cuts and confirm there are no ongoing licensing fees attached to the deliverables.

What is the difference between a product video and a product photo for e-commerce?

A product video shows the product in motion, in use, or in context, communicating benefits that static images cannot. Research consistently shows that product pages with video convert at higher rates than those with photos alone. For paid social ads on Meta, TikTok, and YouTube, video consistently outperforms static creative in cost-per-click and return on ad spend.
