## Summary

Video testimonials build trust faster than written customer reviews because potential customers can see and hear a real person. The easiest way to make them is to start with the reviews you already collect, invite your happiest customers on camera, and publish short videos on your website and social media.

## Key Takeaways

- A video testimonial is social proof in its most convincing form.
- Start with written customer reviews to find the best customer stories.
- Keep each testimonial video short: 30 to 90 seconds for websites, 15 to 30 seconds for social media.
- Publish customer testimonial videos on your landing page, Google Business Profile, and social media.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a customer testimonial video?
A short video in which a real customer describes their experience with a business, product, or service, usually 30 to 90 seconds long.

### Why are video testimonials more effective than written reviews?
Potential customers can see and hear a real person, which builds trust faster than text.

### What makes a customer review video effective?
It shows a real customer, a real problem, and a real result: what was wrong before, why they chose the business, and what changed after.

### Should the customer read a script?
No. Prepare three or four questions and let the customer answer in their own words. Natural answers are more convincing.

### How do businesses find customers for testimonial videos?
Start with customers who already left a five-star review. A review platform that sends review requests after each visit makes this list grow steadily.

### Where should testimonial videos be filmed?
In the real place where the product or service happened, such as the restaurant, the renovated kitchen, or the clinic.

### What should be added in editing?
B-roll of the team at work, captions for silent viewing, and a single clear story.

### How long should a testimonial video be?
Between 30 and 90 seconds for websites, and 15 to 30 seconds for social media.

### Where should businesses publish testimonial videos?
On the landing page near the pricing or booking button, on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, in email campaigns, and on the Google Business Profile.

### Do I need special video testimonial software?
No. A phone and a quiet room can produce a good testimonial. Software helps collect videos at scale; professional production gives the best quality for websites and ads.

### Can one testimonial be reused?
Yes. One video can become a full case study, a short social clip, and a quote card.

### Should stock footage be used in testimonial videos?
No. The value of a testimonial is that the customer is real.
