## Why do restaurants need video marketing in 2026?

Restaurants compete for attention at the exact moment a customer decides where to eat, and in 2026 that decision increasingly happens inside a social media feed or a search result. Static food photos still work, but short video clips of a dish being plated, a dining room filling up on a Saturday night, or a chef narrating a seasonal special outperform photos on every major platform by a wide margin in both organic reach and paid ad performance.

For local restaurants in Long Island, Nassau County, and Suffolk County, video also drives direct-search discovery. Google's local results surface video content from Google Business Profiles, and TikTok's location-based discovery feed has become a go-to restaurant finder for many diners. A restaurant that posts no video is effectively invisible to a large share of its potential customer base.

## What types of videos work best for restaurants?

Five video formats drive the most return for food and restaurant businesses:

- **Hero brand film (60 to 90 seconds):** A cinematic walk through the restaurant, the kitchen, and the ambiance. This runs on the homepage, YouTube, and in retargeting campaigns.
- **Dish showcase (15 to 30 seconds):** A close-up, beautifully lit video of one signature dish. This runs as a paid Meta or TikTok ad and as organic social content.
- **Seasonal promotion (15 seconds):** A quick visual announcing a new menu item, a holiday special, or a limited-time offer. These are the fastest and most affordable to produce.
- **Behind-the-scenes (30 to 60 seconds):** The kitchen, the prep, the team. This format builds trust and personality, and it performs particularly well as an organic reel on Instagram and TikTok.
- **Short paid ad (15 seconds):** A single hook, one offer, one call to action. This is the workhorse of restaurant paid advertising on Meta and TikTok.
