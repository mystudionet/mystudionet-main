How to Use LinkedIn Video Ads to Get More B2B Leads in 2026

LinkedIn video ads reach decision-makers at a lower cost per qualified B2B lead than most paid platforms when set up correctly. Here is the format to use, the hook to write, the budget to set, and the metrics to track.

Kursad Yonet
October 20, 2026

LinkedIn video ads generate more qualified B2B leads per dollar than Facebook or Google for most professional service, consulting, and SaaS businesses when the targeting is tight and the creative is direct. The typical cost per lead runs higher in absolute terms than other platforms, but the lead quality, measured by close rate and deal size, is consistently better for B2B offers. A 15 to 30 second video with a clear offer, targeted by job title and company size, is the baseline that works.

Quick Summary

  • LinkedIn video ads outperform most paid channels for B2B lead generation when targeting decision-makers by job title, company size, or industry
  • The best-performing LinkedIn video ads run 15 to 30 seconds and open with the viewer's exact problem in the first 3 seconds
  • A realistic starting budget for a LinkedIn video campaign is $1,500 to $3,000 per month, with cost per lead typically ranging from $50 to $200 for professional services
  • Most businesses need at least 2 to 3 video assets: a cold-audience awareness ad, a retargeting offer ad, and optionally a proof or case study cut
  • With AI video production, LinkedIn-ready B2B ad packages typically deliver in 1 to 3 weeks at a fraction of traditional shoot cost
  • Long Island and New York businesses targeting other businesses are among the highest-ROI use cases for LinkedIn video advertising

Why do LinkedIn video ads work better for B2B than other platforms?

LinkedIn video ads work better for B2B because the platform's targeting is built around professional identity: job title, seniority level, company size, industry, and specific companies. A New York consulting firm can reach CFOs at manufacturing companies in Nassau County or Suffolk County with a message about their exact problem. Facebook and Google cannot match that precision for professional audiences.

The tradeoff is cost. LinkedIn impressions cost more than other platforms. A $10 CPM on Facebook can become $25 to $50 on LinkedIn. But for a $20,000 services contract, the economics still work because the leads are decision-makers, not curious browsers.

B2B businesses that see the best results with LinkedIn video typically share these traits:

  • Average deal value of $5,000 or more
  • A specific, named audience (not "small business owners" but "operations directors at logistics companies with 50 to 500 employees")
  • A video that addresses a pain the viewer recognizes in the first 3 seconds
  • A lead form or landing page that captures contact details, not just a company homepage

How do you write a LinkedIn video ad that gets clicks from decision-makers?

A LinkedIn video ad that converts opens with the specific pain of the target audience, not a company name or logo. Decision-makers skip branded intros. They stop scrolling for their own problems.

The structure that works in 15 to 30 seconds:

  • Seconds 0 to 3: Name the exact problem. "If you're a law firm still running ads on stock footage..." or "Most New York contractors are losing bids because their pitch has no video."
  • Seconds 4 to 15: Name the cost of the problem and introduce the solution in one sentence. No jargon.
  • Seconds 15 to 25: One proof point or visual demonstration. Show, do not claim.
  • Final frame: A clear call to action with a specific next step, not "learn more."

What budget do you need to start a LinkedIn video campaign?

For a first LinkedIn video campaign, a realistic test budget is $1,500 to $3,000 per month. That range generates enough impressions and clicks to draw meaningful conclusions in a 30-day window. Spending under $1,000 per month on LinkedIn typically produces too few impressions to compare creative or refine targeting.

At that spend level, a B2B services business targeting 50,000 to 150,000 professionals in a specific job title or industry can expect:

  • 3,000 to 8,000 video views per month at a typical LinkedIn CPM
  • 30 to 80 link clicks or lead form opens
  • 5 to 20 qualified leads, depending on offer clarity and targeting precision

Cost per lead benchmarks vary by industry: $50 to $200 for professional services, $100 to $400 for enterprise software, and $30 to $100 for local services targeting a tightly defined geography such as Long Island or Nassau County.

How do you measure whether your LinkedIn video ads are working?

Four metrics tell the real story on a LinkedIn video campaign:

  • Video view rate: the percentage of impressions that resulted in a view of at least 25 percent of the video. Below 15 percent means the thumbnail or first frame is the problem, not the rest of the creative.
  • Click-through rate (CTR): 0.3 to 0.5 percent is typical on LinkedIn. Above 0.5 percent is strong. Below 0.2 percent means the call to action or offer needs revision.
  • Cost per lead (CPL): the only number that ultimately matters, benchmarked against your average deal size. A $150 CPL on a $25,000 service engagement is excellent.
  • Lead quality score: manually verify that the people filling out your form actually match your target job title and company size during the first month of any campaign.

Is LinkedIn video the right channel for Long Island and New York businesses?

LinkedIn video ads are a strong fit for professional services firms, SaaS companies, B2B product brands, consultants, agencies, and any business whose buyer is a working professional at a specific type of company. They are a poor fit for B2C retail, local consumer services, and any offer under $1,000 in deal value, where the platform's CPM makes the economics difficult.

For Long Island businesses specifically, LinkedIn allows geographic targeting by metro area or region. A West Babylon or Hauppauge-based consulting firm can reach CFOs and operations directors across Suffolk County and Nassau County, plus the five boroughs of New York City, without paying for impressions in markets that do not convert.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a LinkedIn video ad be?

LinkedIn video ads perform best at 15 to 30 seconds for cold audiences and 30 to 60 seconds for retargeting. Decision-makers on LinkedIn tolerate more content than a consumer scrolling Instagram, but only if the first 5 seconds address a real problem. Keep the cold-audience version short and save the longer format for people who have already seen your brand.

Do LinkedIn video ads require professional production?

No, but low production quality costs more on LinkedIn than on TikTok or Instagram because the audience evaluates vendor credibility quickly. A poorly lit, poorly edited video signals the same quality as your actual work. AI-produced video, designed and paced correctly, performs as well as traditionally shot content at a significantly lower cost.

What type of call to action converts best on LinkedIn?

Lead generation forms that pre-fill with the viewer's LinkedIn profile data consistently outperform landing page clicks for B2B offers. An offer with a specific, concrete outcome, such as "Book a 20-minute audit" or "Download the 2026 benchmark report," outperforms vague calls to action like "Learn more" or "Contact us."

Can a small Long Island business afford LinkedIn video ads?

Yes, if the deal value supports it. A business closing projects at $5,000 or more per client can justify a $1,500 to $2,500 monthly test budget on LinkedIn, especially when combined with retargeting. Tight geographic and job-title targeting keeps CPM down, and strong creative keeps CTR up. Mystudionet Productions can build the creative component from a free discovery call through final delivery.

How many videos do you need to run a LinkedIn campaign?

A minimum viable LinkedIn video campaign needs two videos: one cold-audience ad that names the problem and the offer, and one retargeting ad that uses social proof or a more detailed case study for viewers who already know who you are. Adding a third variant to A/B test the opening line dramatically improves optimization speed and lowers cost per lead over 60 to 90 days.
