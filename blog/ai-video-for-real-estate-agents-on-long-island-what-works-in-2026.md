## Why do real estate agents on Long Island need video in 2026?

Real estate is one of the most visual purchase decisions a person makes, and Long Island buyers browse dozens of listings online before contacting a single agent. A video walks them through the property and neighborhood in 90 seconds, answering questions that photos cannot: how light moves through the rooms, how close neighboring homes are, what the street actually feels like.

Real estate industry research consistently shows that buyers and sellers are significantly more likely to contact an agent who uses video. On platforms like YouTube and Instagram, properties with listing videos receive more inquiries and spend fewer days on the market compared to photo-only listings.

For agents in Nassau County and Suffolk County, where the housing market is competitive at every price point, video is no longer optional. It is table stakes.

## What types of videos work best for real estate agents in 2026?

Three formats generate the most leads for real estate professionals:

- **Listing walkthrough video:** A cinematic tour of a property, 60 to 120 seconds, designed for YouTube pre-roll, Instagram Reels, and Facebook ads.
- **Neighborhood spotlight video:** A 60 to 90 second lifestyle video showing the community around the property, including schools, parks, and local businesses.
- **Broker brand film:** A 60 to 120 second video introducing the agent or team, their market expertise, and their approach. Builds trust before a buyer or seller ever picks up the phone.
