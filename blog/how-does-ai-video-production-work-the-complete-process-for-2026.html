How Does AI Video Production Work? The Complete Process for 2026

AI video production in 2026 follows a five-step process: discovery call, concept and script, AI generation and scene assembly, revisions, and final delivery. Most projects go from approved brief to finished video in 1 to 3 weeks, at roughly 20 to 40 percent of traditional production cost.

Kursad Yonet
October 06, 2026

AI video production in 2026 works through five main stages: a discovery call to align on goals, a creative brief and script, AI-powered generation and scene assembly, client revision rounds, and final export across all needed formats. From approved concept to delivered video, most projects take 1 to 3 weeks. The cost runs roughly 20 to 40 percent of what a traditional camera crew would charge for the same result.

Quick Summary

  • AI video production has five stages: discovery, brief and script, generation, revisions, and delivery.
  • Typical turnaround is 1 to 3 weeks versus 4 to 8 weeks for a traditional shoot.
  • AI production eliminates location costs, crew day rates, and weather delays.
  • Most professionally produced AI commercials include 2 to 3 revision rounds.
  • Final delivery covers 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 formats for broadcast, Meta, YouTube, and TikTok.
  • AI production is a better fit for scripted, brand-driven content than for founder testimonials or live event coverage.

What happens on a discovery call?

The discovery call is a 30-minute conversation where the studio learns your goals, audience, budget, and timeline. By the end, both sides know whether AI production is the right tool for the project and what a realistic scope looks like.

A good discovery call covers: what the video needs to accomplish, where it will run (Meta, YouTube, broadcast TV, a website), who the viewer is, whether there is existing brand footage or photography, and any mandatory brand guidelines. Studios that skip this step tend to deliver videos that look technically polished but miss the strategic mark.

How does the creative brief and script stage work?

After the discovery call, the studio delivers a creative brief outlining the concept, the visual style, the narrative arc, and the call to action. For most AI commercials, this includes a full script.

The brief typically includes:

  • A one-paragraph treatment describing what the viewer will see and feel
  • A scene-by-scene breakdown (3 to 8 scenes for a 30 to 60 second spot)
  • Visual reference images showing the intended aesthetic
  • A voiceover script or on-screen text plan
  • Platform-specific notes (aspect ratio, safe zones, caption requirements)

Client approval at this stage is what triggers generation. Changes to the concept after generation has started cost time, so the brief is where precision pays off. If you want to understand what makes a script convert before this stage, read how to write a video ad script that actually converts.

How does the AI generation stage work?

This is the stage that looks like magic from the outside and looks like skilled filmmaking from the inside. The studio uses AI generation tools to produce individual scenes from the approved brief, then assembles, paces, and grades them in post-production.

The generation stage includes several passes:

  • Scene generation: each scene is prompted, generated, and evaluated for composition, motion, lighting, and brand fit. A single scene may require several generation attempts to reach the right result.
  • Character and object consistency: if the same AI-generated person or product appears across multiple scenes, the studio uses consistency controls to keep them visually coherent.
  • Assembly and pacing: scenes are cut together in a timeline, timed to the voiceover or music, and refined for rhythm.
  • Color grading: the assembled edit is graded to a consistent cinematic look that matches the brand palette.
  • Sound design: voiceover (recorded or AI-generated), licensed music, and ambient sound are added and mixed.
The AI generation stage combines prompting, scene assembly, and post-production craft.

The output at this stage is an internal rough cut, not a final video. It exists to show the client the assembled concept before revision rounds begin.

How many revision rounds are included and what can be changed?

Most professional AI video packages include 2 to 3 revision rounds. A revision round means the client reviews the current cut, submits consolidated feedback, and the studio addresses it before the next round.

What can be revised after generation:

  • Pacing and edit timing: cuts can be shortened, lengthened, or reordered.
  • Voiceover and script: wording changes, pacing adjustments, and accent preferences.
  • Music and sound mix: track selection and volume balance.
  • Text overlays and graphics: copy, size, position, and animation style.
  • Color and mood: grading adjustments within a defined range.

What typically requires a new generation (and may affect cost):

  • A scene that needs a completely different visual concept
  • A character that needs to be replaced across multiple scenes
  • A fundamental change to the narrative structure after generation has begun

Understanding this boundary is the main practical difference between AI production and traditional editing. Because generating a scene takes time, structural changes after generation cost more than they would in a purely editorial workflow. This is also one of the key differences covered in AI video vs. traditional video production.

What does final delivery include?

Final delivery from Mystudionet Productions includes the master video file plus all requested platform cuts. A typical commercial delivery package covers:

  • 16:9 master: full-resolution broadcast and YouTube format
  • 9:16 vertical cut: for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
  • 1:1 square cut: for Meta feed placements
  • Subtitle/caption version: required by most paid social platforms for sound-off viewing
  • Clean music version: master without voiceover, for markets where voiceover is re-recorded locally

Turnaround from approved rough cut to final delivery is typically 3 to 7 business days. Total project turnaround, including the discovery and brief stages, runs 1 to 3 weeks for a standard commercial. More complex projects with multiple scenes or campaign packages may run 3 to 4 weeks.

Is AI production right for every type of video?

AI production is the strongest fit for scripted, brand-driven commercial content: product videos, brand films, video ads, explainer videos, and social content where the story is told through visuals and voiceover rather than an on-camera person.

AI production is a weaker fit for:

  • Founder or executive testimonials where the business owner's actual face and presence is the message
  • Live event coverage that needs authentic documentary footage
  • Customer testimonial videos that depend on the credibility of a real person speaking on camera
  • Videos that legally require verifiable footage (certain regulated industries)

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to provide footage, photos, or a logo to get started?

You do not need existing footage. A logo and any brand color or typography guidelines are helpful for text overlays, but most AI video productions start from a written brief alone. If you have existing photography or video that you want incorporated, the studio can work with those as reference assets.

What if I do not like the direction after seeing the rough cut?

One structural revision (a significant change to the concept, scenes, or narrative direction) is typically included in the standard package. If the brief was approved and generation followed that brief, a full concept restart would be treated as a new project scope. This is why studios invest time in the discovery and brief stages before generating a single frame.

How is AI video production different from using an AI tool myself?

Professional AI video production combines a generation tool with filmmaking craft: shot design, pacing, color grading, sound design, and revision discipline. Consumer AI tools generate clips; a professional studio turns them into a finished commercial. The difference shows up most in consistency across scenes, cinematic quality, and strategic alignment with a marketing goal.

Can I use the finished video on television?

Yes. Professionally finished AI video is delivered at broadcast-ready specifications and can run on television, streaming services, YouTube pre-roll, Meta placements, and TikTok. Mystudionet Productions delivers platform cuts in the correct specifications for each channel.
