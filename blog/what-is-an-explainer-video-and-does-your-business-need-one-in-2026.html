What Is an Explainer Video and Does Your Business Need One in 2026?

An explainer video is a short video, typically 60 to 90 seconds, that shows exactly what a product or service does and why a customer should care. Most businesses that sell anything complex, technical, or unfamiliar need one.

Kursad Yonet
October 09, 2026

An explainer video is a short video, typically 60 to 90 seconds, that answers one question: what does this product or service do and why should I care? It is not a commercial and it is not a brand film. It is the clearest, fastest way to reduce buyer confusion and move someone from "I don't get it" to "I want that." Most businesses that sell something complex, technical, or unfamiliar need one.

Quick Summary

  • An explainer video runs 60 to 90 seconds and answers one question: what does this do and why do I need it?
  • It is most valuable for software, services, healthcare, financial products, and anything a prospect finds confusing.
  • AI-produced explainer videos in 2026 typically cost in the low to mid four figures, versus the mid to high five figures for a fully animated traditional production.
  • A professionally produced explainer can meaningfully increase landing page conversion rates, which is why it is often the first video a business should make.
  • Turnaround with AI production is 1 to 3 weeks. Traditional animated explainers often take 8 to 12 weeks.

What exactly is an explainer video?

An explainer video is a focused, concise video designed to explain a product, service, or process to a new audience. Unlike a brand film, which tells your company story, an explainer zeroes in on how something works and what problem it solves.

The three most common formats are:

  • Animated: motion graphics, illustrated characters, or screen-recorded UI walkthroughs
  • Live-action: a presenter, a product demo, or a real-world scenario
  • AI-generated: photorealistic scenes produced with AI tools, combining the visual richness of live-action with the speed and flexibility of animation

Most effective explainers share the same structure: open with the customer's problem, introduce the solution, show how it works, and end with one clear call to action.

What kinds of businesses need an explainer video?

An explainer video delivers the most value when your product or service requires explanation before someone will buy it. If a visitor can land on your website and still not understand what you do after reading your homepage headline, an explainer video will pay for itself quickly.

Industries where explainer videos consistently perform well include:

  • Software and SaaS products (what does the dashboard actually do?)
  • Healthcare and medical devices (what does this treatment or device do?)
  • Financial services (how does this account or product work?)
  • Home services (what is the process, step by step?)
  • E-commerce (how is this product used and why is it different?)
  • Professional services including law, accounting, and consulting (what does the engagement look like?)

If you sell something that could be sold with a single photo and a price tag, you may not need an explainer. If your sales team spends the first 10 minutes of every call explaining the basics, you need one immediately.

How much does an explainer video cost in 2026?

AI-produced explainer videos in 2026 typically cost in the low to mid four figures, compared to mid to high five figures for a traditional animated production. That cost gap exists because AI production removes the frame-by-frame illustration, the voiceover booking lag, and the lengthy animation render times that drove up traditional explainer costs.

For context on how AI production compares more broadly, see our posts on how much an AI commercial costs in 2026 and how AI video production works.

What moves the price up or down:

  • Length: a 90-second video costs more than a 60-second one, though the difference is not linear
  • Visual complexity: a simple motion graphics walkthrough costs less than a multi-scene photorealistic narrative
  • Voiceover: a professional voiceover recording adds a modest fixed cost
  • Revisions: additional rounds of changes increase total production time
  • Deliverables: a single 16:9 master costs less than a full package with 9:16 and 1:1 cuts for social platforms
A well-produced explainer video answers your buyer's top question before your sales team picks up the phone.

How long should an explainer video be?

The sweet spot for an explainer video is 60 to 90 seconds. Engagement drops sharply after 90 seconds for explainer-style content across landing pages and social platforms. The goal is to explain the core value, not tell the whole story.

If your product or service genuinely requires more depth, a two-part approach works well: a 60-second overview for the top of the funnel and a longer 2 to 3 minute deep-dive for prospects who are already engaged.

Where do businesses use explainer videos?

An explainer video is one of the most versatile assets a business can own. Once produced, it can be used across every stage of the buyer journey:

  • Website homepage or product page: the single highest-impact placement
  • Sales emails: embedded or linked in outreach sequences to warm up cold leads
  • YouTube and LinkedIn: for B2B brands, LinkedIn video consistently outperforms static posts
  • Meta and Google ads: a 30-second cut from a longer explainer makes a strong awareness ad
  • Trade shows and presentations: plays on loop without needing a live presenter
  • Investor decks and pitch meetings: explains what you do in 90 seconds without relying on slides

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between an explainer video and a commercial?

A commercial is designed to create desire and drive immediate action, usually in 15 to 30 seconds, and is primarily a paid advertising tool. An explainer video is designed to educate and reduce buyer confusion, typically runs 60 to 90 seconds, and lives on a website, in sales emails, and at the top of the funnel. The two serve different purposes, though a strong commercial can include a brief explanatory moment.

How long does it take to produce an explainer video?

With AI production, most explainer videos go from approved script to final delivery in 1 to 3 weeks. Traditional animated explainers with custom illustration and frame-by-frame animation often take 8 to 12 weeks. Does my explainer video script need to be written before the project starts?

No. Mystudionet Productions develops the script as part of the production process. Most clients find that the scripting stage alone clarifies their own messaging.

Can an explainer video be used as a paid ad?

Yes. A 60 to 90 second explainer can be cut into a 15 to 30 second ad for Meta, YouTube, or LinkedIn without a separate production. Most businesses that commission an explainer video ask for an ad cut at the same time to maximize the production investment.

What makes an explainer video fail?

The two most common failure modes are trying to explain too much and starting with the company instead of the customer's problem. An effective explainer opens by naming the viewer's pain, not by introducing the brand. Poor audio is the second: a great visual with muddy or robotic voiceover loses the viewer immediately.
