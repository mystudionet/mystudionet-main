How Long Should a Video Ad Be for Meta, YouTube, and TikTok in 2026?

For Meta (Facebook and Instagram), the ideal video ad runs 15 to 30 seconds. YouTube pre-roll converts best at 15 to 20 seconds. TikTok ads perform strongest between 21 and 34 seconds. Here are the platform rules, what drives ad cost by length, and how to get all formats from one production.

Kursad Yonet
September 29, 2026

For Meta (Facebook and Instagram), the ideal video ad runs 15 to 30 seconds. YouTube skippable pre-roll converts best at 15 to 20 seconds. TikTok ads perform strongest in the 21 to 34 second range. Getting the length wrong is one of the most expensive mistakes in paid social, because platform algorithms penalize poor watch-through rates and charge more per result.

Quick Summary

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): 15 to 30 seconds for feed and Reels ads; 10 to 15 seconds for retargeting.
  • YouTube: 15 to 20 seconds for skippable pre-roll; 6 seconds for bumper ads; up to 3 minutes for in-feed awareness.
  • TikTok: 21 to 34 seconds is the current conversion sweet spot for in-feed ads.
  • LinkedIn: 15 to 30 seconds for lead generation; up to 2 minutes for brand awareness.
  • Every platform rewards a strong hook in the first 3 seconds, regardless of total length.
  • A single AI-powered production can deliver all platform cuts from one core shoot.

Why does video ad length actually matter?

Platform algorithms use watch-through rate as a quality signal. An ad that holds 60 percent of viewers to the end earns lower cost-per-result over time compared to a longer ad with 20 percent completion, even if the longer version carries more information.

On Meta, shorter ads are cheaper per completed view. On YouTube, a viewer can skip after 5 seconds, so an ad that buries the message past that mark gets abandoned before it pays off. On TikTok, the native feed rewards ads that match the rhythm of organic content. Length is not just a viewer preference. It is a budget and performance variable.

How long should a Meta video ad be in 2026?

For most Meta campaigns, 15 to 30 seconds is the right range. Retargeting ads shown to warm audiences who already visited your site can be shorter, 10 to 15 seconds, because the viewer already knows your brand. Cold prospecting ads benefit from slightly more time, 20 to 30 seconds, to build context before the call to action.

Meta placement rules by format:

  • Feed ads (Facebook and Instagram): 15 to 30 seconds. Square 1:1 or portrait 4:5 performs better than horizontal 16:9 in the feed.
  • Reels ads: 15 to 30 seconds in vertical 9:16 format. Native feel outperforms repurposed broadcast cuts.
  • Stories ads: 15 seconds maximum per card in vertical 9:16.
  • In-stream ads: 5 to 15 seconds for skippable placements; up to 60 seconds for non-skippable, though shorter still converts better.

Mystudionet Productions typically delivers Meta packages as a 30-second hero cut plus a 15-second variant, so you have both the full story and a fast retargeting version from one production run.

How long should a YouTube video ad be in 2026?

YouTube has four ad formats with different length ceilings, and matching the format to your goal matters more than picking an arbitrary length.

  • Skippable in-stream (TrueView): Viewers can skip after 5 seconds. Industry performance data consistently shows 15 to 20 seconds outperforms longer versions for conversion campaigns. For brand awareness, up to 60 seconds is acceptable if the content earns the watch.
  • Non-skippable in-stream: Capped at 15 to 20 seconds by Google depending on region. You cannot make these longer.
  • Bumper ads: 6 seconds, non-skippable. Best for brand recall and retargeting where you only need a reminder.
  • In-feed ads (formerly discovery): These appear in search results and alongside other videos. Longer form, 2 to 3 minutes, can work here because the viewer actively chose to click.

For most Long Island businesses running YouTube pre-roll, the practical starting point is a 15 to 20 second skippable ad. A 6-second bumper cut from the same footage adds an affordable retargeting layer.

One production run delivers all platform cuts: 30-second hero, 15-second variant, and 6-second bumper.

How long should a TikTok video ad be in 2026?

TikTok's own performance reports put the conversion sweet spot at 21 to 34 seconds. This surprises many marketers who assume shorter always wins, but TikTok's audience is conditioned to watch content rather than scroll static images. A hook-driven story that builds to a clear offer in that window matches native viewing behavior.

TikTok ad format rules:

  • In-feed ads: 9 to 60 seconds supported, with 21 to 34 seconds consistently outperforming shorter and longer cuts in conversion campaigns.
  • TopView ads: Up to 60 seconds. Reserved for high-budget brand launches.
  • Branded Hashtag Challenges: 9 to 15 seconds works well here since participation is the goal, not a direct click.

A TikTok ad is not a vertical crop of a broadcast spot. It needs text overlays, an immediate hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds, and pacing that matches how organic TikTok content moves. A repurposed 30-second horizontal ad will underperform a native vertical edit of the same message.

What about LinkedIn video ads?

For LinkedIn, 15 to 30 seconds works for lead generation. Up to 2 minutes is acceptable for brand awareness or thought leadership. LinkedIn's audience is in a professional context and tolerates slightly more depth than Instagram, but conversion ads should still front-load the value proposition in the first 5 seconds.

LinkedIn video is underused by most small and mid-size businesses in professional services, finance, healthcare, and B2B sectors across Nassau County and Suffolk County. It is often a lower cost-per-lead entry point for reaching decision-makers compared to Meta, for the right offer.

Should you produce separate videos for each platform?

Not separate shoots, but separate edits. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal video length for Facebook ads?

The industry consensus for Facebook and Instagram feed ads is 15 to 30 seconds. Retargeting ads can run 10 to 15 seconds because the audience already recognizes your brand. The most critical rule is that your hook must stop the scroll in the first 3 seconds before length becomes relevant.

Can I use the same video on every platform?

Technically yes, but performance suffers. Each platform has different aspect ratios, viewer expectations, and algorithm signals. A 30-second horizontal broadcast cut repurposed to TikTok without a native vertical edit and strong opening text will consistently underperform against a video made for that platform. Budget for platform edits from the start.

Does video length affect ad cost on Meta and YouTube?

Yes. On Meta, shorter ads with high completion rates tend to earn lower cost-per-result over time because the algorithm rewards engagement signals. On YouTube TrueView, you pay when a viewer watches 30 seconds or the full ad if it is shorter. A 15-second ad where the viewer watches all 15 seconds costs the same per view as a 60-second ad where they watch 30, but the 15-second version is cheaper to produce and easier to keep tight.

What is the best starting point for a small business with a limited video ad budget?

Start with a single 15 to 20 second video cut formatted for Meta feed and YouTube pre-roll. That one asset covers the two highest-volume paid social placements. Once it proves out, add a 6-second bumper for YouTube retargeting and a TikTok-native vertical edit. This three-asset set covers the most-used formats without overcomplicating the first production.

How many platform cuts does Mystudionet Productions include in a typical campaign?

Most AI-produced campaigns at Mystudionet Productions include the core hero spot plus up to four platform cuts, typically the 15-second Meta variant, the 20-second YouTube cut, the TikTok-native edit, and the 6-second bumper. Specific deliverables are agreed during the discovery call based on which platforms the campaign runs on.
