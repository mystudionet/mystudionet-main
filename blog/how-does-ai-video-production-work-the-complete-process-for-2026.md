## What happens on a discovery call?

The discovery call is a 30-minute conversation where the studio learns your goals, audience, budget, and timeline. By the end, both sides know whether AI production is the right tool for the project and what a realistic scope looks like.

A good discovery call covers: what the video needs to accomplish, where it will run (Meta, YouTube, broadcast TV, a website), who the viewer is, whether there is existing brand footage or photography, and any mandatory brand guidelines. ## How does the creative brief and script stage work?

After the discovery call, the studio delivers a creative brief outlining the concept, the visual style, the narrative arc, and the call to action. For most AI commercials, this includes a full script.

The brief typically includes:

- A one-paragraph treatment describing what the viewer will see and feel
- A scene-by-scene breakdown (3 to 8 scenes for a 30 to 60 second spot)
- Visual reference images showing the intended aesthetic
- A voiceover script or on-screen text plan
- Platform-specific notes (aspect ratio, safe zones, caption requirements)

## How does the AI generation stage work?

The generation stage includes several passes: scene generation (each scene is prompted, generated, and evaluated), character and object consistency controls, assembly and pacing in a timeline, color grading, and sound design. The output at this stage is an internal rough cut shown to the client before revision rounds begin.

## How many revision rounds are included and what can be changed?

Most professional AI video packages include 2 to 3 revision rounds. A revision round means the client reviews the current cut, submits consolidated feedback, and the studio addresses it before the next round.

What can be revised after generation:

- Pacing and edit timing
- Voiceover and script wording
- Music and sound mix
- Text overlays and graphics
- Color and mood (within a defined range)

Structural changes (replacing scenes or changing narrative direction) after generation has started may affect cost.

## What does final delivery include?

Final delivery from Mystudionet Productions includes:

- 16:9 master (broadcast and YouTube format)
- 9:16 vertical cut (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)
- 1:1 square cut (Meta feed placements)
- Subtitle and caption version
- Clean music version (without voiceover)

Turnaround from approved rough cut to final delivery is typically 3 to 7 business days. Total project turnaround runs 1 to 3 weeks for a standard commercial.

## Is AI production right for every type of video?

AI production is the strongest fit for scripted, brand-driven commercial content: product videos, brand films, video ads, explainer videos, and social content. It is a weaker fit for founder testimonials, live event coverage, customer testimonials, and videos that legally require verifiable footage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to provide footage, photos, or a logo to get started?

You do not need existing footage. A logo and any brand color or typography guidelines are helpful for text overlays, but most AI video productions start from a written brief alone. If you have existing photography or video, the studio can work with those as reference assets.

### What if I do not like the direction after seeing the rough cut?

One structural revision is typically included in the standard package. If the brief was approved and generation followed that brief, a full concept restart would be treated as a new project scope. This is why studios invest time in the discovery and brief stages before generating a single frame.

### How is AI video production different from using an AI tool myself?

Professional AI video production combines a generation tool with filmmaking craft: shot design, pacing, color grading, sound design, and revision discipline. Consumer AI tools generate clips; a professional studio turns them into a finished commercial.

### Can I use the finished video on television?

Yes. Professionally finished AI video is delivered at broadcast-ready specifications and can run on television, streaming services, YouTube pre-roll, Meta placements, and TikTok. Mystudionet Productions delivers platform cuts in the correct specifications for each channel.
