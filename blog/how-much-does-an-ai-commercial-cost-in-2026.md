# How Much Does an AI Commercial Cost in 2026? | Mystudionet Productions

> AI commercial production costs explained by Mystudionet Productions, a Long Island AI video studio: typical price ranges, what drives pricing up or down, and how AI compares to traditional commercial production budgets.

## Summary

AI-produced commercials cost dramatically less than traditional production — typically 20–40% of an equivalent traditional shoot. A traditionally produced 30-second commercial runs $20,000–$60,000 for local quality and well past $100,000 for national quality, because traditional production is a logistics business: crews, locations, equipment, and reshoots. AI production removes most of those logistics costs.

## Typical AI Commercial Price Ranges in 2026

- **Short social media ad (15–30s):** entry four figures
- **Full commercial spot (30–60s, broadcast quality):** mid four figures to low five figures
- **Campaign packages (multiple versions/formats/platforms):** priced per deliverable set, typically below the cost of one traditional shoot day

## What Drives AI Commercial Pricing

- Creative complexity (single scene vs. multi-scene narrative)
- Character consistency across shots (requires additional craft and iteration)
- Revision depth
- Number of deliverables (16:9, 9:16, 1:1 platform cuts)
- Voiceover and music licensing
