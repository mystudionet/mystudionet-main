## How much does a traditional commercial cost?

A traditionally produced 30-second commercial routinely costs $20,000 to $60,000 for a local business, and well past $100,000 for national quality, before any media spend. That budget pays for a director, camera crew, lighting and sound teams, location fees, actors, equipment rental and weeks of post-production.

Those costs exist because traditional production is a logistics business. You pay to move people and equipment to a physical place, capture footage under time pressure, and fix everything else in the edit.

## How much does an AI commercial cost in 2026?

Most professionally produced AI commercials in 2026 fall into three ranges:

- **Short social media ad (15 to 30 seconds):** entry four figures, comparable to what a single day of traditional crew time used to cost.
- **Full commercial spot (30 to 60 seconds, broadcast quality):** mid four figures to low five figures, depending on complexity.
- **Campaign packages (multiple versions, formats and platforms):** priced per deliverable set, and still typically below the cost of one traditional shoot day.

AI production removes most of the logistics. There is no location to rent, no crew to feed and no reshoot when the weather turns. What remains is the part that always mattered most: the idea, the craft and the execution. The pattern holds across projects: commercial-grade results at roughly 20 to 40 percent of traditional cost.

## What makes an AI commercial more or less expensive?

Five factors move the quote, and creative complexity matters most:

- **Creative complexity:** a single-scene product showcase costs less than a multi-scene story with recurring characters.
- **Character consistency:** keeping the same AI-generated actor across many shots takes additional craft and iteration.
- **Revision depth:** more rounds of refinement mean more generation and edit time.
- **Deliverable count:** one hero video costs less than a full package of platform cuts (16:9, 9:16 and 1:1).
- **Voiceover and music licensing:** professional voiceover and licensed tracks add modest fixed costs.
