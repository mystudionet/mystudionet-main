## What does a product video typically cost in 2026?

In 2026, professionally produced product video costs fall into a few clear ranges based on production method:

- **Short social ad (15 to 30 seconds, AI production):** low four figures
- **Full product video (30 to 90 seconds, AI production):** mid four figures to low five figures
- **Traditional product video (30 to 90 seconds):** $15,000 to $50,000 for a local shoot, higher for national quality
- **Multi-product campaign (several formats and platforms):** priced per deliverable set, still well below a single traditional shoot day

AI production removes most of the logistics expense: no rented studio, no crew to schedule, no travel, and no reshoot when lighting does not cooperate. What remains is concept, craft, and precise product representation.

## What type of product video does a small business actually need?

The format you choose affects cost more than length does. Common product video formats for small businesses:

- **Product demo:** shows the product in use, typically 30 to 60 seconds, works well on YouTube and Meta
- **Lifestyle video:** shows the product in a real-world context to communicate brand feel, 15 to 60 seconds
- **E-commerce product video:** tight, clean presentation for a product detail page or Amazon listing, often 30 to 45 seconds
- **Social media ad:** cut for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts, usually 15 to 30 seconds in 9:16 format
- **Launch film:** a longer, more cinematic piece (60 to 90 seconds) for a new product introduction

A small business that sells one or two products typically starts with one hero video plus two or three platform cuts.

## What drives the price of a product video up or down?

Five factors move the quote most significantly:

- **Product complexity:** a single hero product with clean packaging films faster than a product with many variants or fine details.
- **Creative direction:** a basic white-background product demo costs less than a cinematic lifestyle video with multiple scenes.
- **Number of platform formats:** one 16:9 video costs less than a full package of 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 cuts.
- **Revision rounds:** more rounds of refinement add generation time and edit hours.
- **Voiceover and licensed music:** professional voiceover and cleared music tracks add a modest but real cost.

## Is AI product video quality good enough for e-commerce and paid ads?

Yes, for most small business applications. In 2026, AI-generated product videos meet the quality bar for Meta ads, YouTube pre-roll, TikTok ads, LinkedIn, Amazon product pages, and website embedding. The gap between a well-crafted AI video and a traditionally produced video has narrowed to the point where most viewers cannot distinguish between them on a phone screen.

AI production is a weaker fit for products where the real tactile feel is the selling point (some luxury goods, handmade items) or shoots that specifically need a well-known real person on camera.

## Who is a product video a good or bad fit for?

A product video is a strong investment for brands selling a physical product that want to increase conversion rates, brands running paid ads on Meta, TikTok, or YouTube, and visually driven categories: food and restaurant, beauty and fashion, health and fitness, home and garden, and retail.

It is a weaker fit when the buying decision is driven almost entirely by spec sheets or when the total budget falls under the typical minimum for a professional production.
