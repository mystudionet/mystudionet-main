## Why do product videos outperform static images for online shoppers?

Product videos outperform static images because they answer the three questions every online buyer silently asks: What does it look like from every angle? How does it work? Can I picture myself using it? A well-produced video answers all three in under 30 seconds.

The effect is strongest in categories where physical properties matter, including apparel, home goods, beauty products, electronics, and food. Brands selling on Amazon and Shopify have reported conversion lifts in the 30 to 80 percent range after adding video to primary product listings.

## What types of product videos convert best on Meta, TikTok, and YouTube in 2026?

**Meta:** 15 to 30 second videos, 1:1 or 9:16. Hook in the first 2 seconds, product in use by second 5, offer in the final 5 seconds.

**TikTok:** 21 to 34 second videos, 9:16. Native-looking style, but AI production can match this tone while maintaining brand consistency.

**YouTube pre-roll:** 15 to 20 second skippable ads. Hook must land before the 5-second skip point.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can AI product videos be used on Amazon and Shopify?

Yes. AI-generated product videos are delivered as standard MP4 files and meet the technical specifications for Amazon A+ content, Amazon video ads, Shopify product pages, and most other e-commerce platforms. Mystudionet Productions delivers files in all required resolutions and aspect ratios.

### How many products can be covered in one production cycle?

Most AI production cycles can handle two to six products in a single brief, depending on how different each product looks and how many format variations are needed per SKU. Briefing multiple products together keeps the visual style consistent and is more efficient than handling them one at a time.

### What does the revision process look like?

A standard product video project includes two rounds of revisions. The first addresses major creative direction including scene, pacing, and tone. The second covers finishing details such as color, music, and text overlays. Additional rounds are available but may affect timeline and cost.

### Do I need to send my product to the studio for AI production?

Not always. AI production can work from high-quality product photos, brand assets, and a detailed brief in many cases. For products where real close-up footage matters, such as textured materials or intricate mechanisms, a short remote-capture session or physical sample may be requested.

### How long does it take to get a finished product video?

Most AI product video projects at Mystudionet Productions deliver a final cut within 1 to 2 weeks of an approved brief, compared to 4 to 6 weeks for a traditional shoot. Rush timelines are sometimes available depending on schedule.
