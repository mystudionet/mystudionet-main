FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Mystudionet Productions do?

Mystudionet Productions is an AI-powered video production studio based on Long Island, New York. We create brand films, TV commercials, video ads, social media content, product videos, and cinematic wedding films for businesses worldwide.

How does AI-powered video production work?

We use cutting-edge generative AI tools at every stage of production — from concept and storyboarding to scene creation and post-production. This allows us to deliver cinematic-quality video faster and at a lower cost than traditional production methods.

Do you work with clients outside of Long Island?

Yes. While we are based on Long Island, NY, we work with businesses from anywhere in the world. Our AI-powered workflow allows us to collaborate remotely and deliver professional video content to clients across the US and internationally.

How much does video production cost?

Pricing varies depending on the type of video, length, and scope. Wedding films start at $2,400. For brand films, commercials, and business video, we offer custom quotes. Contact us for a free consultation.

How long does it take to produce a video?

Timelines depend on the project. A social media video can be turned around in days. A full brand film typically takes 2–4 weeks from brief to delivery. AI-powered production significantly reduces timelines compared to traditional methods.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with businesses of all sizes — from local Long Island businesses to national brands and startups. Our clients span industries including food & restaurant, real estate, healthcare, technology, retail, and more.

How do I get started?

Simply fill out our contact form or call us at (631) 355-5588. We offer a free discovery call to discuss your project goals, timeline, and budget.
