### What style of wedding films does Mystudionet Productions make?
Mystudionet Productions creates cinematic wedding films with a documentary-influenced aesthetic — capturing genuine moments, real emotions, and authentic storytelling rather than staged or performative content. Every Mystudionet wedding film is original in structure, not a template.

### Does Mystudionet film elopements and micro-weddings?
Yes. Mystudionet Productions offers elopement and micro-wedding film packages for couples who are celebrating with a small guest list or an intimate ceremony. These packages can be tailored to the scope and setting of the event. Contact the studio to discuss options for smaller celebrations.

### What wedding video package does Mystudionet recommend?
Most couples choose a package that includes full-day coverage (ceremony through reception dancing), a cinematic highlight film of 3–7 minutes, and digital delivery. Mystudionet Productions offers additional options including a full-length film with extended footage, same-day edits delivered at the reception, and additional raw footage access. The recommended package depends on the couple's priorities and the length of their celebration.
