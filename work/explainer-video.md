## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is an explainer video?
An explainer video is a short video — typically 60–120 seconds — that explains a product, service, concept, or process in a simple, engaging, and visually compelling way. Explainer videos are used on home pages, product pages, sales decks, email campaigns, and paid advertising. They are particularly effective for businesses with complex offerings that benefit from visual demonstration.

### How long should an explainer video be?
Research consistently shows that explainer videos perform best at 60–90 seconds for most applications. At this length, the video can clearly communicate the problem, the solution, the key benefits, and the call to action without losing viewer attention. For more complex technical subjects, 2–3 minutes may be appropriate. Mystudionet Productions recommends the appropriate length based on the subject and distribution platform.

### What makes a good explainer video script?
A strong explainer video script follows a proven structure: hook with the problem the target audience faces, introduce the solution, explain how it works (key features/benefits), social proof or credibility, and a clear call to action.
