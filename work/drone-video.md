### Is drone video legal everywhere on Long Island?
Drone operations are subject to FAA regulations, local ordinances, and airspace restrictions. Certain areas of Long Island — including areas near airports (Islip MacArthur, Republic, JFK, LaGuardia, and others) — require airspace authorization before drone operations can be conducted. Mystudionet Productions manages all necessary authorizations and operates in full compliance with FAA regulations. The studio will advise clients if a specific location has restrictions that affect drone availability.
