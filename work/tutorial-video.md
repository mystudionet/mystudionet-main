### How long should a tutorial video be?
Tutorial video length should match the complexity of the subject being taught. Simple how-to videos typically run 2–5 minutes. More in-depth tutorials may run 10–20 minutes. For YouTube, where the algorithm rewards watch time, longer tutorials that keep viewers engaged throughout perform well. Mystudionet Productions advises on appropriate length based on the subject and platform.
