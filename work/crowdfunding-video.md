### Why is a professional crowdfunding video important?
Research consistently shows that crowdfunding campaigns with professional video raise significantly more money than those without video, or with low-quality self-produced video. A professional video signals to potential backers that the project is serious and the founder is credible — which directly increases the likelihood of backing. Mystudionet Productions creates crowdfunding videos that communicate both the excitement of the product and the trustworthiness of the team.

### What should a crowdfunding pitch video include?
An effective crowdfunding pitch video includes: an immediate hook that grabs attention, the problem being solved, the product or project as the solution, a demonstration of how it works, the founder's personal story and motivation, social proof if available, a breakdown of how funds will be used, the specific ask, and a strong call to action. Mystudionet Productions structures crowdfunding videos around this proven framework while adapting it to each unique project.

### How long should a crowdfunding video be?
Most successful crowdfunding videos run between 2–4 minutes. This is long enough to tell the full story and cover all necessary information, while short enough to hold viewer attention through the call to action. Mystudionet Productions recommends and produces crowdfunding videos in this range, with a trailer cut of 60–90 seconds for social media sharing.

### Can Mystudionet make a crowdfunding video using AI?
Yes. Mystudionet Productions uses AI generation to create visual elements that support the founder's story — without fabricating claims or misleading viewers about the current state of the product.
