Mystudionet's YouTube ad productions are built around this critical five-second window.

### How long should a video ad be?
Optimal video ad length varies by platform and objective. For Facebook and Instagram, 15–30 seconds typically performs best for awareness and lead generation. TikTok ads perform well at 15–60 seconds. YouTube skippable ads need to hook in the first 5 seconds, with the main message ideally in the first 15–30 seconds. YouTube non-skippable ads are capped at 15–20 seconds. Mystudionet Productions produces video ads in all required lengths and can deliver multiple length variations from a single production.
