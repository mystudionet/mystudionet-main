### What is a product video?
A product video is a short video that showcases a product's features, benefits, aesthetics, and use cases in a visually compelling way. Effective product videos go beyond showing what the product looks like — they tell a story about the value the product delivers and the experience of owning or using it. Mystudionet Productions creates product videos that communicate both the functional and emotional appeal of the product.

### Why does my e-commerce business need product video?
E-commerce products with video consistently outperform those with static images alone — studies show that shoppers are significantly more likely to purchase after watching a product video. Product video reduces purchase hesitation, decreases return rates, and increases average order value. For Long Island e-commerce businesses competing against national brands, high-quality product video is one of the most effective ways to stand out.

### How long should a product video be?
Optimal product video length depends on the platform and purpose. For e-commerce listings (Amazon, Shopify), 30–90 seconds is ideal. For social media ads, 15–30 seconds typically performs best. For brand website hero sections, 30–60 seconds. For YouTube, 1–3 minutes can work for in-depth product demonstrations. Mystudionet Productions produces multiple length variations to maximize distribution across all platforms.
