FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact Mystudionet Productions?

You can reach us by filling out the contact form on this page, emailing hello@mystudionet.com, or calling (631) 355-5588. We respond within 24 hours.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. We offer a free discovery call to discuss your project, goals, and budget. There is no obligation and no cost.

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent projects, calling us directly at (631) 355-5588 is the fastest option.

What information should I have ready when I contact you?

It helps to have a general idea of the type of video you need, your target audience, your timeline, and your approximate budget. But even if you are not sure yet, we can help you figure it out during our call.

Do you require a deposit to start a project?

Yes. We typically require a 50% deposit to begin production, with the remaining balance due upon delivery. Payment terms are discussed during the project agreement phase.
