### What types of videos work best for nonprofits?
The most effective nonprofit video types include: mission films (communicating the organization's purpose and impact), beneficiary stories (putting a human face on the cause), fundraising appeal videos (driving donations during campaigns or galas), impact report videos (showing donors how their contributions made a difference), volunteer recruitment videos, and awareness campaign videos for social media and broadcast.

### How does video help a nonprofit raise more money?
Video fundraising appeals consistently outperform text and image-only appeals in response rate and average donation size. Donors respond to stories — and video is the most powerful format for delivering a story that moves people to give. Nonprofits that use professionally produced video in their fundraising campaigns, on their websites, and in their donor communications consistently raise more money than those that rely on static content alone.
