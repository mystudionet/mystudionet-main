### Why do medical practices need professional video?
Patients increasingly research healthcare providers online before making an appointment — and the practices that communicate most effectively online capture the most new patients. A professionally produced physician introduction video creates familiarity and trust before the first visit, reducing patient anxiety and increasing the likelihood of booking. Practice brand films communicate the care philosophy and patient experience in a way that written content cannot.

### Does healthcare video need to comply with regulations?
Healthcare marketing video is subject to HIPAA guidelines regarding patient privacy, as well as medical board regulations governing medical advertising in New York State. Mystudionet Productions is aware of these requirements and works with healthcare clients to ensure all video content is compliant. Patient testimonials require proper authorization, and all clinical claims in video must meet applicable standards.
