# Data Deletion Instructions | Mystudionet Productions

> How to remove the Mystudionet app from Instagram and ask Mystudionet Productions (Blipmedia Inc.) to delete all data about your connected account within 30 days. Full page: https://mystudionet.com/data-deletion

## Summary

To delete your data, first remove our app in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. Then email hello@mystudionet.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your Instagram username, or call (631) 355-5588. We delete the access token, account ID, username, profile picture, stored media and captions, and your contact details within 30 days and send a confirmation with a reference number. Posts already published stay on your Instagram account until you delete them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I remove the Mystudionet app from Instagram?**
Open your profile, tap the menu, choose Settings and activity, open Apps and websites and tap Remove next to our app.

**What happens when I remove the app?**
Our access stops immediately. We can no longer publish on your account or read any information from it.

**How do I ask for my data to be deleted?**
Email hello@mystudionet.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your Instagram username, or call (631) 355-5588.

**What data is deleted?**
The access token, Instagram account ID, username, profile picture, stored media files and captions, and your contact details unless you are still an active client.

**How long does deletion take?**
Within 30 days of your request.

**Will I get a confirmation?**
Yes. We email a confirmation with a reference number when deletion is complete.

**Are my published Instagram posts deleted too?**
No. Published posts stay on your account. You can delete them in Instagram yourself.

**Do I have to be a current client to ask for deletion?**
No. Anyone whose account was connected to our app can ask.

**Is there a cost to delete my data?**
No, deletion is free.

**Where can I read what data you collect?**
In the Privacy Policy at https://mystudionet.com/privacy

**Who handles deletion requests?**
Mystudionet Productions, a brand of Blipmedia Inc., Long Island, New York.
