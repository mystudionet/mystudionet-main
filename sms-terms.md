> Text message (SMS) program terms for Mystudionet Productions (Blipmedia Inc.), a video production company on Long Island, New York. Last updated October 16, 2026. Full page: https://mystudionet.com/sms-terms

## Summary

Mystudionet Productions, a brand of Blipmedia Inc., sends text messages only to people who opt in. Messages include inquiry replies, appointment and shoot-date reminders, project updates and occasional offers about our video production services. Message frequency varies, typically up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I opt in to Mystudionet text messages?**
Check the SMS consent box on the contact form at https://mystudionet.com/contact, or text START or JOIN to (631) 515-6611. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

**What messages will I receive?**
Replies to your inquiry, appointment and shoot reminders, project updates and occasional promotional offers.

**How often will I get messages?**
Frequency varies, typically up to 4 messages per month.

**Does it cost anything?**
Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

**How do I stop the messages?**
Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT. You will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to join again.

**How do I get help?**
Reply HELP, email hello@mystudionet.com or call (631) 355-5588.

**Is my phone number shared?**
No. Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent, will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

**Are carriers responsible for missed messages?**
No. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
