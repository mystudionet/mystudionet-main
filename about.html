I'm the founder and director behind Mystudionet Productions. For over 20 years, I've been telling stories through a lens — first with traditional cinema techniques, now with the most advanced AI filmmaking tools available anywhere.

My Approach

AI is a Tool.
Story is the Product.

When AI filmmaking tools emerged, I was one of the first filmmakers in the region to integrate them into professional production. Not as a gimmick — but because they genuinely make the work better.

AI handles what was once the slow, expensive part: pre-visualization, scene generation, rapid iteration. I handle what AI can't: the instinct for the right moment, the understanding of emotion, the director's eye that makes a film feel human.

The result is a studio that moves at the speed of a startup and delivers at the quality of a legacy production house — at a price that makes sense for Long Island businesses and couples.
