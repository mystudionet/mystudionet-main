# Privacy Policy | Mystudionet Productions

> How Mystudionet Productions (Blipmedia Inc.) collects, uses and protects information in its social media management service, including Instagram publishing. Last updated October 5, 2026. Full policy: https://mystudionet.com/privacy

## Summary

Mystudionet Productions is a brand of Blipmedia Inc., a video production and social media company on Long Island, New York. Business clients connect their own Instagram professional account to our app so we can publish content they have approved. We receive the account ID, username, account type, profile picture and an access token from Meta. We never receive the account password, we never read private messages and we never collect follower data. We use the information only to publish and report on approved posts. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who runs the Mystudionet app?**
Mystudionet Productions, a brand of Blipmedia Inc., based on Long Island, New York.

**What information does the app receive from Instagram?**
The Instagram account ID, username, account type, profile picture and an access token issued by Meta when the account owner authorizes the app.

**Does Mystudionet get my Instagram password?**
No. The account owner logs in directly with Instagram. We only receive an access token.

**What is the information used for?**
To publish photos, videos and captions the client has approved, at the time the client chose, and to confirm the result.

**Does Mystudionet read direct messages or follower data?**
No. The app does not request access to messages or follower lists.

**Is my data sold or used for ads?**
No. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

**Where is the data stored?**
Access tokens are kept in encrypted storage on our secure servers. Media files are hosted on Cloudflare so Instagram can fetch them.

**Who else sees the data?**
Only the providers needed to run the service: Meta, Cloudflare and our server host, plus authorities if the law requires it.

**How long is data kept?**
While the client uses the service. After disconnection or cancellation, tokens and account data are deleted within 30 days. Billing records of published posts are kept up to 12 months.

**How do I disconnect the app?**
In Instagram, go to Settings, then Apps and websites, and remove the app. Access stops immediately.

**How do I ask for my data to be deleted?**
Follow the steps at https://mystudionet.com/data-deletion or email hello@mystudionet.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

**Is the service meant for children?**
No. It is a business service and is not directed to children under 13.

**How will I know if the policy changes?**
The date at the top of the policy is updated, and clients are emailed about significant changes.
