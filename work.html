FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of videos are in the Mystudionet portfolio?

Our portfolio includes AI-powered brand films, TV commercials, video ads, product videos, testimonial videos, social media content, drone footage, and cinematic wedding films.

Can I see samples of your work before hiring you?

Yes. Our portfolio page showcases selected projects across different categories. You can also request additional samples relevant to your industry or video type during a consultation.

Do you produce videos for specific industries?

Yes. We have produced videos for businesses in food & restaurant, real estate, healthcare, technology, beauty, retail, education, automotive, and many other sectors.

Can I request a video similar to one I saw in your portfolio?

Absolutely. If you see a style or format you like in our portfolio, let us know and we will discuss how to adapt that approach for your brand and goals.

Do you own the rights to the videos you produce?

Upon full payment, clients receive full ownership and usage rights to their videos. We retain the right to showcase the work in our portfolio unless a confidentiality agreement is in place.
